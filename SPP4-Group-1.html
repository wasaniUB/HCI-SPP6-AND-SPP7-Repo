Store It

Know where every lab item is before you step into the storeroom.

Problem & Solution

Science students waste time hunting through multiple storerooms for misplaced or used-up supplies, and supervisors find out about shortages too late. Store It links every shelf and bottle to a mobile scanning app, so anyone can find items instantly, log usage in seconds, and get low-stock alerts before supplies run out.

Core Tasks

  1. Looking for Items Simple
    Find a specific chemical without searching through multiple rooms.

  2. Updating Records and Notifying Medium
    Keep the shared inventory accurate after taking, moving, or using up an item.

  3. Alerts on Low Supply Complex
    Notice that a supply is running low and restock it before anyone runs out.
